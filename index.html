Our Accommodation

Choose from our variety of comfortable and well-equipped living spaces designed to meet the needs of modern students.

Standard Shared Dormitory

Our shared dormitories offer a comfortable and affordable living option with all essential amenities for students who enjoy community living.

  • Comfortable single bed with mattress
  • Personal study desk with ergonomic chair
  • Spacious wardrobe with secure locker
  • High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Shared bathroom (4:1 ratio)
  • 24/7 power backup
Starting at ₹8,000/month
Premium Shared Dormitory

Upgraded shared accommodation with additional space and premium features for students seeking extra comfort and privacy.

  • Larger single bed with foam mattress
  • Expanded study area with bookshelf
  • Walk-in wardrobe with full-length mirror
  • Ultra-high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Shared bathroom (2:1 ratio)
  • Individual reading lamp and power outlets
Starting at ₹12,000/month
Guest House Suite

Premium accommodation for visiting faculty, parents, and guests with hotel-like amenities and services.

  • Queen-size bed with luxury linens
  • Private attached bathroom
  • Walk-in wardrobe with full-length mirror
  • Study/work desk with executive chair
  • Flat-screen TV with satellite channels
  • Mini refrigerator and tea/coffee maker
  • Daily housekeeping service
Starting at ₹2,500/night

Our Facilities

Silicon Hostel provides comprehensive facilities to ensure a comfortable, safe, and enriching living experience for all residents.

Common Lounge Area

Spacious common areas designed for relaxation and social interaction among residents.

24/7 Medical Support

On-campus medical facility with ambulance service for emergencies.

Sky Labs

Dedicated study spaces with high-speed internet and resource materials.

High-Speed Internet

Campus-wide fiber optic Wi-Fi with dedicated bandwidth for uninterrupted connectivity.

Dining Services

Nutritious meals prepared under strict hygiene standards with varied menu options.

Fitness Center

Well-equipped gym with cardio and strength training equipment for resident use.

Study Rooms

Quiet study spaces with individual carrels and group study rooms available 24/7.

Security

24/7 security with CCTV surveillance and controlled access to all buildings.

Laundry

Modern laundry facilities with washing, drying, and ironing services available.
